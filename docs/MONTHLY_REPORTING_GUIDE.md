# Monthly SEO and Analytics Reporting Guide

## Overview

This guide provides a comprehensive framework for generating monthly SEO and analytics reports for AskScrooge. These reports track performance, identify trends, and guide optimization efforts.

## Report Schedule

- **Report Generation**: First business day of each month
- **Report Period**: Previous calendar month
- **Distribution**: By end of first week of month
- **Recipients**: Marketing team, leadership, stakeholders

## Monthly Report Structure

### 1. Executive Summary (1 page)

Provide a high-level overview of key metrics and performance.

#### Key Metrics to Include:

- **Organic Traffic**
  - Total organic users (vs. previous month)
  - Total organic sessions (vs. previous month)
  - Month-over-month change (%)

- **Search Performance**
  - Total impressions (vs. previous month)
  - Total clicks (vs. previous month)
  - Average CTR (vs. previous month)
  - Average position (vs. previous month)

- **Conversions**
  - Total conversions (vs. previous month)
  - Conversion rate (vs. previous month)
  - Revenue (if applicable)

- **Key Highlights**
  - Top performing page
  - Top search query
  - Biggest improvement
  - Areas of concern

#### Template:

```
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - [MONTH] [YEAR]

Organic Traffic: [X] users (+[Y]% vs. previous month)
Search Impressions: [X] (+[Y]% vs. previous month)
Conversions: [X] (+[Y]% vs. previous month)

Key Highlights:
- Top page: [Page name] with [X] views
- Top query: "[Query]" with [X] impressions
- Best improvement: [Metric] increased [Y]%
- Area of concern: [Metric] decreased [Y]%
```

### 2. Traffic Analysis (2-3 pages)

Analyze organic traffic trends and sources.

#### Sections:

1. **Organic Traffic Trend**
   - Line chart showing daily/weekly organic users
   - Highlight peaks and valleys
   - Identify trends

2. **Traffic by Source**
   - Breakdown of traffic sources:
     - Organic search (Google, Bing, etc.)
     - Direct traffic
     - Referral traffic
     - Social traffic
   - Pie chart showing distribution

3. **Traffic by Device**
   - Desktop vs. mobile vs. tablet
   - Device-specific trends
   - Mobile optimization opportunities

4. **Traffic by Country**
   - Top 10 countries by traffic
   - Geographic trends
   - Localization opportunities

5. **New vs. Returning Users**
   - Percentage of new users
   - Percentage of returning users
   - Trend over time

#### Analysis Questions:

- Is organic traffic increasing or decreasing?
- Which traffic sources are most valuable?
- Are mobile users engaging well?
- Which geographic regions are most active?
- What is the new user acquisition rate?

### 3. Search Performance (2-3 pages)

Analyze search engine performance from Google Search Console.

#### Sections:

1. **Search Performance Overview**
   - Total impressions
   - Total clicks
   - Average CTR
   - Average position
   - Trend over time

2. **Top Search Queries**
   - Top 20 queries by impressions
   - Top 20 queries by clicks
   - Top 20 queries by CTR
   - Identify high-potential keywords

3. **Top Landing Pages**
   - Top 10 pages by impressions
   - Top 10 pages by clicks
   - Top 10 pages by CTR
   - Identify high-performing pages

4. **Search Visibility Trend**
   - Line chart showing impressions over time
   - Line chart showing clicks over time
   - Identify seasonal patterns

5. **Keyword Opportunities**
   - Keywords with high impressions but low CTR
   - Keywords with high position but low clicks
   - Keywords with growth potential

#### Analysis Questions:

- Is search visibility increasing?
- Which keywords are driving the most traffic?
- Which pages are ranking well?
- Are there keywords with high potential?
- What is the average ranking position?

### 4. Page Performance (2-3 pages)

Analyze performance of individual pages.

#### Sections:

1. **Top Performing Pages**
   - Top 10 pages by views
   - Top 10 pages by engagement
   - Top 10 pages by conversions
   - Identify what makes them successful

2. **Page Engagement Metrics**
   - Average engagement time by page
   - Bounce rate by page
   - Scroll depth by page
   - Identify engagement patterns

3. **Page Conversion Performance**
   - Pages with highest conversion rate
   - Pages with most conversions
   - Pages with lowest conversion rate
   - Identify optimization opportunities

4. **New Pages Performance**
   - Performance of pages added this month
   - Comparison to site average
   - Identify successful new content

5. **Underperforming Pages**
   - Pages with lowest engagement
   - Pages with highest bounce rate
   - Pages with no conversions
   - Identify pages needing optimization

#### Analysis Questions:

- Which pages are most valuable?
- Which pages need optimization?
- What content resonates with users?
- Are new pages performing well?
- What is the average engagement time?

### 5. Conversion Analysis (1-2 pages)

Analyze conversion performance and funnel.

#### Sections:

1. **Conversion Overview**
   - Total conversions
   - Conversion rate
   - Revenue (if applicable)
   - Trend over time

2. **Conversion by Source**
   - Conversions by traffic source
   - Conversion rate by source
   - Identify most valuable sources

3. **Conversion by Device**
   - Conversions by device type
   - Conversion rate by device
   - Identify device-specific issues

4. **Conversion Funnel**
   - Funnel visualization
   - Drop-off rates at each step
   - Identify bottlenecks

5. **Conversion Trends**
   - Line chart showing conversions over time
   - Identify seasonal patterns
   - Compare to previous months

#### Analysis Questions:

- Is conversion rate improving?
- Which sources drive the most conversions?
- Where are users dropping off?
- Are there device-specific issues?
- What is the average conversion value?

### 6. Content Performance (1-2 pages)

Analyze content strategy and performance.

#### Sections:

1. **Content by Type**
   - Performance by content type (blog, product, etc.)
   - Identify most valuable content types

2. **Content Engagement**
   - Average engagement time by content type
   - Bounce rate by content type
   - Scroll depth by content type

3. **Content Conversion**
   - Conversion rate by content type
   - Conversions by content type
   - Identify high-converting content

4. **Content Gaps**
   - Topics with high search volume but no content
   - Keywords with high potential
   - Content opportunities

5. **Content Calendar**
   - Planned content for next month
   - Content optimization priorities
   - Content promotion strategy

#### Analysis Questions:

- Which content types perform best?
- What topics resonate with users?
- Are there content gaps?
- What content should be optimized?
- What new content should be created?

### 7. Technical SEO (1 page)

Monitor technical SEO health.

#### Sections:

1. **Indexing Status**
   - Valid pages
   - Pages with warnings
   - Excluded pages
   - Pages with errors

2. **Mobile Usability**
   - Mobile-friendly pages
   - Pages with issues
   - Issue breakdown

3. **Core Web Vitals**
   - LCP (Largest Contentful Paint)
   - FID (First Input Delay)
   - CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)
   - Trend over time

4. **Crawl Errors**
   - 404 errors
   - Server errors
   - Redirect errors
   - Blocked resources

5. **Security Issues**
   - Security issues detected
   - Manual actions
   - Spam issues

#### Analysis Questions:

- Are all pages indexed?
- Are there mobile usability issues?
- Are Core Web Vitals meeting targets?
- Are there crawl errors?
- Are there security issues?

### 8. Competitive Analysis (1 page)

Compare performance to competitors.

#### Sections:

1. **Keyword Rankings**
   - Keywords where you rank #1
   - Keywords where competitors rank #1
   - Keywords with growth potential

2. **Traffic Comparison**
   - Estimated traffic vs. competitors
   - Traffic growth rate comparison
   - Market share analysis

3. **Backlink Analysis**
   - Number of backlinks
   - Quality of backlinks
   - Backlink growth

4. **Content Comparison**
   - Content volume comparison
   - Content quality comparison
   - Content strategy comparison

#### Analysis Questions:

- How do we rank vs. competitors?
- What keywords are competitors targeting?
- What is our market share?
- What backlinks do competitors have?
- What content strategy are competitors using?

### 9. Recommendations (1-2 pages)

Provide actionable recommendations for next month.

#### Sections:

1. **Quick Wins**
   - Easy optimizations with high impact
   - Timeline: 1-2 weeks
   - Expected impact

2. **Content Opportunities**
   - New content to create
   - Existing content to optimize
   - Content gaps to fill

3. **Technical Improvements**
   - Technical issues to fix
   - Performance optimizations
   - Mobile improvements

4. **Strategy Adjustments**
   - Keyword strategy changes
   - Content strategy changes
   - Promotion strategy changes

5. **Priorities for Next Month**
   - Top 3 priorities
   - Expected impact
   - Timeline

#### Template:

```
RECOMMENDATIONS - [MONTH] [YEAR]

Quick Wins:
1. [Recommendation] - Expected impact: [X]% increase in [metric]
2. [Recommendation] - Expected impact: [X]% increase in [metric]

Content Opportunities:
1. Create content for "[Keyword]" - Search volume: [X]
2. Optimize "[Page]" for "[Keyword]" - Current position: [X]

Technical Improvements:
1. Fix [Issue] - Affects [X] pages
2. Optimize [Performance metric] - Current: [X], Target: [Y]

Priorities for Next Month:
1. [Priority] - Expected impact: [X]%
2. [Priority] - Expected impact: [X]%
3. [Priority] - Expected impact: [X]%
```

### 10. Appendix (1-2 pages)

Include detailed data and charts.

#### Sections:

1. **Detailed Metrics Table**
   - All metrics with month-over-month comparison
   - Percentage change
   - Trend indicator

2. **Charts and Visualizations**
   - Traffic trend chart
   - Conversion trend chart
   - Top pages chart
   - Top queries chart

3. **Data Sources**
   - Google Analytics 4
   - Google Search Console
   - Other tools used

4. **Methodology**
   - How metrics are calculated
   - Data collection period
   - Any limitations or caveats

## Report Generation Process

### Step 1: Gather Data (1-2 hours)

1. Export data from Google Analytics 4
   - Traffic metrics
   - Conversion metrics
   - Engagement metrics
   - Page performance

2. Export data from Google Search Console
   - Search performance
   - Top queries
   - Top pages
   - Coverage status

3. Export data from other tools
   - Rank tracking tools
   - Backlink analysis tools
   - Competitor analysis tools

### Step 2: Analyze Data (2-3 hours)

1. Calculate month-over-month changes
2. Identify trends and patterns
3. Compare to benchmarks
4. Identify opportunities and issues
5. Prepare visualizations

### Step 3: Create Report (2-3 hours)

1. Write executive summary
2. Create sections with analysis
3. Add charts and visualizations
4. Include recommendations
5. Proofread and format

### Step 4: Review and Distribute (1 hour)

1. Review report for accuracy
2. Get feedback from team
3. Make revisions
4. Distribute to stakeholders
5. Schedule follow-up meeting

## Report Metrics Reference

### Traffic Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Organic Users | Unique users from organic search | Increase 10-20% MoM |
| Organic Sessions | Sessions from organic search | Increase 10-20% MoM |
| Bounce Rate | % of sessions that bounced | < 50% |
| Avg Session Duration | Average time per session | > 2 minutes |

### Search Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Impressions | Times site appears in search | Increase 5-10% MoM |
| Clicks | Clicks from search results | Increase 10-15% MoM |
| CTR | Click-through rate | > 3% |
| Avg Position | Average ranking position | < 10 |

### Conversion Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Conversions | Total conversions | Increase 10-20% MoM |
| Conversion Rate | % of sessions that converted | > 2% |
| Revenue | Total revenue from conversions | Increase 15-25% MoM |
| Cost per Conversion | Cost to acquire one conversion | Decrease 5-10% MoM |

### Engagement Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Engagement Rate | % of engaged sessions | > 50% |
| Events per Session | Average events per session | > 3 |
| Pages per Session | Average pages per session | > 2 |
| Scroll Depth | % of page scrolled | > 50% |

## Best Practices

1. **Be consistent**: Use same metrics and format each month
2. **Focus on trends**: Look for patterns over time
3. **Compare periods**: Always compare to previous month
4. **Provide context**: Explain why metrics changed
5. **Include recommendations**: Provide actionable insights
6. **Use visualizations**: Charts are easier to understand
7. **Keep it concise**: Focus on key insights
8. **Tell a story**: Connect metrics to business goals
9. **Take action**: Use insights to optimize
10. **Track impact**: Measure results of recommendations
